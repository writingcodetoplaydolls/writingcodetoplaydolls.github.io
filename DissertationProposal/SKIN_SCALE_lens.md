## 3. The Floating Skin Project

`K:\ResearchRepository\FloatingSkinProject`, 23,743 files, two parts: `3DScanning` and `FloatingSkinSoftware_AugmentedVersion_Javascript`.

From your own `docs/00-overview-and-rationale.md`:

> "We are not trying to 'get a scan.' We are trying to produce a **reusable skin asset** — the *floating skin* — from a human subject (face first, body later), at the highest practical fidelity, that can be re-used for: **real-time applications** (games/AR/avatars)... **3D printing**: needs dense, watertight, manifold, metric-scaled geometry, usually **shelled** (given a wall thickness so it prints as a skin, not a solid head)."

> "These requirements conflict. The design resolves the conflict by **separating geometry from detail**."

Hardware: Samsung S21 for photogrammetric detail, RealSense D435 for metric scale, Kinect v2 for body. Registered to **FLAME** (Faces Learned with an Articulated Model and Expressions), chosen because it models "shape + expression + head pose + neck" rather than neutral shape alone.

Three docs, 2,057 words total: overview and rationale, hardware inventory, master pipeline.

I have not read the JavaScript side.
