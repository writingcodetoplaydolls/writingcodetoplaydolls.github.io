# Floating Skin — motion viewers

Each page is a self-contained 3D viewer (Three.js loads from a CDN — no other files needed).
Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom. Every page has a **clip picker** (Dance / Motion dropdown) and a
**Both / Out / In wind toggle**. Two bodies are offered where noted: **stock** (clean SMPL‑X figure)
and **pregnant** (the 3D scan).
