## Trancorporeality

*Sunsets for Mike and Carmen*, 2016. Generative Contributory Animation, stereo sound. Running time: Endless. [vimeo.com/191740479](https://vimeo.com/191740479)

Seven revolutions through an endless generative loop of radial slit scan sunset images.

Sunsets for Mike and Carmen is a generative radial slit scan comprised of social media sunsets. In August of 2016 my friend Mike took his own life. He had recently moved to Florida with his wife Carmen to start over and live the life they had worked so hard for. Almost every day he posted pictures of the sunset slowly slipping down into the Gulf of Mexico. These sunsets were evidence of his relentless spirit and a welcome respite from the banality of social media. I was desperate to do something for Carmen to help ease her pain so I decided Mike’s sunsets should live on as a small gift to her. This piece uses a set of Mike’s original images and is designed to incorporate new contributed images. As   watch the sun rise and take in the view,   cannot keep the experience. It is never the same twice. Similarly, each individual sunset generated is unique and fleeting. As long as the code runs, new sunsets will be sent into the world.

During exhibition users are invited to take new sunset images and text them to me. Every morning the new images will be added to the que and incorporated into the freshly generated new sunsets.

## Assemblages as memory keepers, as understanding

**\[Note\]** The corpus begins with the potato bin on   mother’s 110 camera (1982–85, in 1.1), then the Mamiya photographs of the kite and the kitchen. The past works fold into the corpus as evidence that theory and abstraction have always been   diffraction grating.

I provided several examples of art installation as the dissertation to cite as examples for my committee, though my research will go far beyond any of those listed, to become a fully multi modal, transdisciplinary, interactive, multi-layered, generative installation that lives in and out of digital space via a website I create that acts as the corpus, the body. My dissertation will fold past work into the current corpus/body as evocative that I have always lived/thought/worked with theory/abstraction as a diffraction grating/lens, dynamic component of my nomadic subjectivities. the previous work begins with the potato bin. the art work also weaves together my subjectivities, mother, artist, photographer, computational person.
