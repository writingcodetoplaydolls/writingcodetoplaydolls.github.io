## 6. Limits on every absence claim in this project

**These are not citation errors; they are the conditions under which the folder's absence findings were measured, and they should be stated whenever one is used.**

**369 of the 1,242 PDFs in `K:\OneDrive\UNM\Dissertation\todo October 26` and `K:\OneDrive\UNM\Comps` were never searched by content**, so every count-based absence claim made before 29 Oct 2026 excludes about 30 per cent of the corpus. **The reason first given for this was wrong.** They were reported as having no text layer; in fact **350 of the 369 have perfectly good native text that `pdftotext` could not reach**, because 254 of them sit at a path longer than 255 characters and 92 have non-ASCII characters in the path. **Only 19 are real scans.** The recovery script is `K:\OneDrive\UNM\Dissertation\todo October 26\_ocr_and_extract.py` and the per-file record is `K:\OneDrive\UNM\Dissertation\todo October 26\TEXT_RECOVERY_manifest.csv`. **Re-run any count-based absence claim against the recovered corpus before relying on it.** For the nineteen that were genuinely OCR'd, the output is uncorrected machine reading and **must be checked against the page image before anything from it is quoted**.

**`Comps\compsnotused` was never searched until 29 October**, and searching it found nine texts the plan listed as missing or did not list at all, among them **Neimanis's *Bodies of Water*, Haraway's *Modest_Witness*, Butler's *Bodies That Matter*, Braidotti's *Nomadic Theory* and *Posthuman Feminism*, and Kirby's *Telling Flesh***. **The pattern is now established four times over: an absence finding in this project has usually been a claim about what had been searched.** Re-run any such claim before writing it down.

**The `Comps` folder itself was outside the original scope**, which the 27 October scope correction records.

**Counts in the close reads include notes, reference lists and figure captions** unless stated otherwise, so a count of 1 or 2 is usually a citation and not an engagement; and whole-word matching was used, so plurals and inflections are undercounted.
