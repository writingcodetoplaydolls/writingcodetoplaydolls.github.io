Sound

  • The quilt videos are silent: Quilt, Quilt2, the four quilt-[…] renders and quilt8purple carry no audio track. anim3.mp4 (the sky) has stereo AAC; TraylorAnimation.mp4 has mono AAC.
  • The house-project clips all have audio: Cell Division, LeapToy, SunsetsforCarmenandMike mono, green1 and purple1 stereo.
  • Neither scene has an AudioSource. The cameras carry AudioListeners, and Unity's VideoPlayer plays a clip's own track by default, so in Unity the sky animation and Cell Division would have been heard.
  • No sound files in Family or TryTeleportAgain. The Sound Art project has piano notes, a metronome and rain wavs. Your libraries: K:\Fodder\Sounds (553 files) and K:\Fodder\AudioBlocks (103).
  • In the web cuts the quilt loops and anim3-sky were encoded without audio; the house clips keep theirs and play muted until the panel's sound toggle. Re-cutting anim3-sky with its stereo track is one ffmpeg flag.
