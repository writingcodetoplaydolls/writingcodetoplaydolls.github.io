## The history section

The history ran about 6,200 words in v35, across Origin Stories, When the Human Got Out of the Way, and The Mechanism of Suppression (pp. 7–22). The final's version (pp. 11–15, with the fold and Cézanne moved to pp. 21–22) is about a third as long. The biggest loss is the argument that organized it: wherever a thinker pointed toward entanglement, "the Platonic inheritance arrived to domesticate the insight … and handing back a binary" (p. 19). The final keeps that as one sentence through Ferrando (final p. 9) and rebuilds the history around two critiques instead.

The final also added history v35 lacked: the contrast with transhumanism, Braidotti's two critiques, Ferrando's list of what the universal human assumed, and postmodernism losing track of the material (final pp. 11–15). A restored history would combine both versions.

For you this history is not background. It is the genealogy of a violence: the erasure of complexity, the insistence on the word and the one truth, and the erasure of the female body. That moves it from 2.1 into 2.4, the gap the dissertation answers, and into 1.2: a location formed by that exclusion, for someone whose knowing comes second or third through the word and lives fully through proprioception. The suppression argument (p. 19) is the spine to restore, and the threads in the table are its evidence:

- **Erasure of complexity:** the suppression pattern itself; Leibniz flattened by Boole; Copenhagen domesticating Bohr; the clinamen reduced to random deviation.
- **The word and the one truth:** Galileo's nature speaking mathematics; Kant's transcendental anthropology; Kuhn's paradigms and Haraway's god trick; Foucault's "man."
- **Erasure of the female body:** theology needing matter dead; Descartes's split of mind from matter; Rich's *Of Woman Born* ("Patriarchal thought has limited female biology to its own narrow specifications"); Mąkowska; Bennett's ecofeminist teachers.
- **What kept surviving:** Spinoza's minor tradition, Bergson, Cézanne, Dada to Black Mountain to Cage, "Diving into the Wreck," and the closing claim that the origin story begins wherever someone dove toward the thing itself.

The computing and art threads also stay in 1.3, as the lineage of a practice built from code, scans, and generative work.

## Cut for good reason

Some of the shrinkage was repetition or correction, and that material is better left where it is.

- **Repetition.** v35 prints "What Counts as Research, Evidence, Knowledge" twice (pp. 24–25 and 26–27). It repeats Bolt and Kirby, Andersen's reading, Franinović's seeds, and Pirrò two or three times each, and pp. 74–75 duplicate p. 73 word for word.
- **Claims the final corrected.** v35 calls Trinh's 1990 work prior to Haraway's 1988 situated knowledges; the final fixes the chronology (final p. 20). "Walking, talking matter" became the correct "walking, talking minerals" (final p. 20).
- **Claims a committee would challenge.** The heading "Deleuze Called for a Diffractive Practice" claims something Deleuze never says. "Noise carries the most information" will draw a technical objection. The Push Back section's "feature, not a bug" answer to researcher-centrism brushes off a fair critique; the final's version, which leaves transferability as an open tension, is easier to defend.
- **Drafting debris.** Notes to yourself in the body (p. 33), "\[page TK\]" placeholders, and "Wikipedia, 2026" as a source for materialism.
