A dedicated volume, a methodology paper, and Sullivan's own part-title. Everything I said about this term being undefined was an artefact of searching the wrong folder.

### The Russian Formalist lineage, and where "making strange" actually sits

174 hits in 39 documents, **and the top two are yours**: the comprehensive exam at 23 and `Comps_Draft_v48` at 22. After that, Schwab 10, **Braidotti's *Nomadic Theory* 7**, **Haraway's *Modest_Witness* 6**, and Andersen's *Making Magic Machines* 5.

So it is your term, and the support is real but thin, with Braidotti's *Nomadic Theory* the nearest thing to a home for it.

### Trinh has a whole book here

933 hits in 24 documents, and **394 of them are in one: Fuser (2019), *Nomadism in the Cinema of Trinh T. Minh-ha*, 72,988 words.** Your own comps exam carries 82, `Comps_Draft_v48` 96.

For section 2.4 this changes the position considerably. You are not working from Trinh's own texts plus a gap in the genealogy; you have a monograph on her that joins her to nomadism.

### Quantum and Whitehead are the best-sourced things in the whole corpus

6,524 quantum hits in 146 documents, 1,271 Whitehead in 64, and there is **a subfolder called `Epperson on Whitehead`** holding the page-split *Quantum Mechanics and the Philosophy of Alfred North Whitehead*: 809, 406, 310, 238, 238 hits across its parts.

Then **Barad, *Meeting the Universe Halfway*, 738**, her chapter 7 "Quantum Entanglements" at 284 (twice), and **Colman, "Quantum Feminicity" 253**.

I had been treating the quantum ground as thin. In Comps it is one of the two densest things you hold.

### Transcorporeality has its source

237 hits, and the sources are exactly right: **Alaimo's own "Trans-corporeality" chapter 41**, **Alaimo & Hekman (eds.), *Material Feminisms* (2008) 41**, the *Posthuman Glossary* 55, Braidotti's *Posthuman Feminism* 14, Sauzet 20.

### Duchamp is in the Schwab volume

73 hits there, which is the highest of any Duchamp document in Comps. Then **Ouellette (2014), *Conditions Variable: Assemblage Theory and Systems Theory in Creative Practice* 30**, Wolfe's *What Is Posthumanism* 8, and **Bolt, *Art Beyond Representation* 6** (held three times).

So Duchamp arrives in your corpus through artistic research and assemblage theory, not through art history. Molderings, in the other folder, is the art-historical one.

## 3. What holds

### Autism

**48 hits in 16 documents across 7.6 million words**, and the top two are **Wolfe, *What Is Posthumanism* at 24** and **Hayles, *How We Became Posthuman* at 7**. Everything else is one or two mentions.

**"Stim" or "stimming": 6 hits in 6 documents.** Proprioception: 21 in 5.

### Appalachia

So my original finding was right for Comps: within this corpus Haraway is the only substantive Appalachian siting. The thing that overturned it, Thorp's Appalachian State dissertation, is in `todo October 26`, not here.

Your section 2.6 remains the least supported part of the draft across both corpora combined.
