## 3. What is free, from the same project, and close to the same argument

## 4. One honest limit on all of this

I have not read the camera chapter. Everything above is its verified citation, its availability, and its project context. **What it actually argues about the camera as apparatus is unknown to me,** and so is whether it does what Ravisankar's one-line reference implies. The judgement that it is the nearest existing treatment to your Chapter 1 opening rests on the title and the project, not on the text.

Worth knowing before pulling it: the camera in their study is a **research instrument handed to girls**, a participatory visual method read diffractively. Your 110 camera is **your own, in your own history**. Those are different relations to the apparatus. The chapter may turn out to be a precedent for the move, or a contrast case for it. Both are useful; they are not the same use.
