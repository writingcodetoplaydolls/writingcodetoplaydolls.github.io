Tests

Small scripts, each recreating one feature in Three.js.

  • Capoeira

    A scanned figure, rigged in Mixamo, running capoeira clips. Click her to switch moves.

  • House

    The SketchUp house and the scanned treehouse under a physically based sky with sun shadows.

  • Reach

    No clips: drag a sphere and the hand reaches for it, solved on the joints every frame by a two-bone solve with a pole, within joint limits measured on the rig and around a solid body; feet too if you like, and in a headset the controllers carry the spheres.

  • Puppet

    The capoeira movement with the Mixamo skin thrown away: every joint is a plain object addressed by name, and a body of spheres and struts, a lamp in one hand, a trail from the other and two sliders that bend joints are written in code on top of it.

Works

Free-standing pieces: entire previous works and proto-assemblages.

  • Proto-assemblage

    The Unity house scene rebuilt: day-night sun, interior lights, the rock and Greg, a video projector on the living-room wall, teleport by clicking a floor.

  • Family

    The December 2018 Unity scene: giant figures, a lamp, an oven and a hawk all wearing quilt animations under a video sky, and the city house with a doorway cut in, a hall inside and a projector throwing out of it.
