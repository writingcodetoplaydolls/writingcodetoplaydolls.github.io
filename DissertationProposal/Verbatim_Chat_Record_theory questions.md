Philosopher Rosi Braidotti defines cartography as a theoretically-based and politically-informed reading of the present. Relying heavily on Spinoza's distinction between two types of power, a Braidottian cartography splits its focus into a dual task:

### 1. Mapping *Potestas* (Power as Entrapment)
This is the component that resembles a traditional mapping of power. It tracks how institutional power operates to marginalize, restrict, and govern bodies. 
* **The Goal:** To establish a rigorous "politics of location". This means assessing exactly where a subject is situated—materially, technologically, and historically—within the complex, advanced capitalist structures of the present day.

### 2. Activating *Potentia* (Power as Empowerment)
This is where Braidottian cartography splits decisively from a purely descriptive map. *Potentia* is Spinoza's concept of an affirmative, vitalist force—the capacity for action, expression, and joy.
* **The Goal:** A Braidottian map does not just state where you are trapped; it actively seeks out the leaks, lines of flight, and spaces for creative resistance. It treats the map as a dynamic **figuration**—a living, performative tool to spark alternatives and hypothesize "the virtual" (what *could* be) rather than just validating the actual status quo.

| Feature | Just a "Mapping of Power" | Braidottian Cartography |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Lens** | Critical / Analytical | Both Critical and **Affirmative** |
| **View of Subject** | Passive product of structures | **Nomadic subject** endowed with agency and relationality |
| **Core Function** | Diagnosing domination (*potestas*) | Transforming entrapment into **empowerment** (*potentia*) |
| **Temporal Focus** | Anchored heavily in past/present structures | Geared toward producing **new futures** and becomings |

In short, a Braidottian cartography maps power explicitly so that it can find out exactly how to subvert it. It insists that critique is useless if it is not combined with the active creation of theoretical and lived alternatives.
