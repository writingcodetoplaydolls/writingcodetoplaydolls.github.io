In short

  • The house itself was built in one month: 2 to 27 March 2019, from the SketchUp model on a Saturday to the final MainScene save 25 days later, with the big pushes on 2, 12, 14, 21, 26 and 27 March.
  • Three follow-up sessions touched the same project: webcams and the night light (28–30 April 2019), keyboard movement (13 July 2019), and Rotate3D plus the Embryos spin-off (2 September 2019).
  • The projector approach outlived the house: the same VideoProjector prefab reappears in 4by3 (March 2019), Embryos (September 2019) and We Have No Room (March 2020).
  • Before the house there was a separate thread, Sept–Nov 2018: the Chunity/ChucK music environment with quilt and sky videos, which became the Family project in Feb 2019.
  • The house-with-video idea predates the SketchUp house. K:\Unity\Family (folder created 2019-02-23) is a copy of a scene last saved 2018-12-09: a downloaded city_house_01 model (added 2018-11-20) with three scans of Flannery standing (2018-11-26 and 2018-12-07), a hawk, nine VideoPlayer components playing the quilt animations and TraylorAnimation.mp4 (Aug 2018), and a video skybox through a render texture (2018-12-07). Also in the folder: a lamp, rocking chair, whales, a wrestling figure, an old chair, a metal oven and a washing machine, all gathered 2018-05-13 to 2018-11-26. The original project it was copied from is not on the drive; only the Feb 2019 copy survives. One stray New Material.mat was added 2019-03-14 18:07, a minute after the House project's last touch, so both were open that evening.
